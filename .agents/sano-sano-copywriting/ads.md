## COPY PRINCIPLES BY PLATFORM

### Google Ads
- **Tone:** Direct, benefit-focused, action-oriented
- **Length:** Short (headlines 30 chars, descriptions 90 chars)
- **Focus:** Pain point + solution + CTA
- **Example:** "Boisson Sans Alcool Premium → Pureté Botanique → Découvrir"

### Meta Ads
- **Tone:** Lifestyle, aspirational, narrative-driven
- **Length:** Medium (enough to tell a story but not overwhelming)
- **Focus:** Emotional connection + brand positioning
- **Example:** "Tu prends quoi ?" / "Sano Sano." (Moment, not feature)

## CONVERSION FUNNEL & LOOKALIKE AUDIENCES

1. **Cold Traffic (New):** Discovery ads (video, carousel) → Brand awareness
2. **Warm Traffic (Visited):** Retargeting with offer → Conversion focus
3. **Hot Traffic (Added to cart):** Urgency messaging → Checkout
4. **Lookalike Audiences:** Use purchasers to find similar users (Meta, Google)
