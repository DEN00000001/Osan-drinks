# Sano Sano — Brand Guidelines

---

## BRAND IDENTITY

### Positioning
**Sano Sano** is a premium non-alcoholic botanical beverage brand for adults (25-45) who refuse to compromise on taste, quality, or celebration.

**Core Promise:** *"Zéro compromis. 100% festif."*

This is not a substitute. This is an authentic choice.

---

## VISUAL IDENTITY

### Color Palette

**Primary:**
- **Background:** Cream/Beige (#F5F3F0 or similar warm neutral)
- **Text/Headlines:** Black (#1A1A1A) or very dark charcoal
- **Accent:** Soft sage green (suggested: #8B9D6F or similar, used for botanical/natural accent elements)

**Photography Tones:**
- Warm, natural lighting
- Earthy, organic color palette
- Professional, sophisticated photography style
- Lifestyle imagery showing celebration moments

### Typography

**Serif Font (Primary):**
- Font family: Garamond or similar serif (classical, premium aesthetic)
- Use for: All headlines, section titles, brand logo
- Weight: Regular and Bold (for emphasis)

**Italic Font (Descriptive):**
- Use serif italic for poetic descriptions, tasting notes, promotional copy
- Creates sophistication and sensory appeal
- Example usage: "Quand l'herbacée franc rencontre l'épice subtile"

**Body Text:**
- Clean, readable sans-serif (e.g., system fonts: -apple-system, Segoe UI, or similar)
- Size: Minimum 16px for body text
- Line height: 1.6+ for readability
- Color: Charcoal/black on cream background

### Design Principles

**Minimalism & Whitespace:**
- Generous margins and padding
- Content-focused, not cluttered
- Breathing room around text and images
- No unnecessary decorative elements

**Visual Hierarchy:**
- Large serif headlines command attention
- Poetic italic subheadings provide context
- Body text supports understanding
- Clear distinction between sections

**Subtle Decorative Elements:**
- Minimal bubble motifs (if any)
- Botanical line illustrations (minimal)
- Photography as primary visual element
- Negative space as design tool

### Photography Style

**Product Photography:**
- Clean, well-lit bottle shots
- Minimal background (cream, white, or soft blur)
- Show carbonation/effervescence where visible
- Professional, high-quality images

**Lifestyle Photography:**
- Celebration moments: apéritifs, dinners, social gatherings
- Authentic, candid moments
- Diverse, urban aesthetic (25-45 demographic)
- Warm, natural lighting
- Show Sano Sano as centerpiece in social setting

**Sourcing/Production Photography:**
- Farm and botanical imagery (e.g., Foster Farm)
- Natural, ethical sourcing narrative
- Process documentation (subtle, not clinical)
- Professional quality, warm tones

## BRAND VOICE & TONE

### Core Characteristics
- **Premium & Sophisticated:** Refined language, no compromises on quality
- **Poetic & Sensory:** Italic, descriptive language for taste and experience
- **Authentic & Direct:** No marketing clichés, honest positioning
- **Celebratory & Assertive:** "Zero compromis. 100% festif" attitude

### Messaging Principles

**What We Say:**
- Focus on purity, authenticity, botanical origin
- Emphasize the exclusive "dynamisation" process
- Describe moments of celebration, not compromise
- Use sensory language (taste, aroma, texture)

**What We Don't Say:**
- "Alternative," "substitute," or "compromise" language
- Health/wellness claims without qualification
- Artificial or chemical references (highlight the opposite)
- Apologetic tone ("better than...")

### Voice Examples

**Premium, Poetic:**
> "Quand l'herbacée franc rencontre l'épice subtile."

**Assertive, Celebration-Focused:**
> "Zéro compromis. 100% festif."

**Authentic, Direct:**
> "Pas une alternative. Une vraie célébration."

**Sensory, Descriptive:**
> "Attaque florale, corps délicat, finale épicée — la pureté botanique révélée."

## PRODUCT IDENTITY

### Basilic-Gingembre

**Visual Identity:**
- Bottle color: Green or emerald tint (suggesting basil, herb origin)
- Label design: Minimalist, serif typography, basil leaf imagery

**Sensory Profile:**
- *Attaque:* Fresh, herbaceous, verdant
- *Corps:* Subtle spice, warm ginger undertone
- *Finale:* Floral, clean, lingering herbal

**Messaging:**
> "Quand l'herbacée franc rencontre l'épice subtile."
> "Fresh, herbaceous, subtle spice, floral finish"

**Moments:** Apéritif urbain, conversation, sophisticated dinner opener

### Thym-Fraise

**Visual Identity:**
- Bottle color: Rose/mauve tint (suggesting strawberry/thyme blend)
- Label design: Minimalist, serif typography, strawberry/thyme imagery

**Sensory Profile:**
- *Attaque:* Fruity, bright, strawberry-forward
- *Corps:* Herbaceous depth, balance of sweet and botanical
- *Finale:* Noble bitterness, clean finish, botanical echo

**Messaging:**
> "L'inattendu qui ravit : sucré et herbacé en harmonie."
> "Fruity with herbaceous depth, noble bitterness, clean finish"

**Moments:** Dessert moment, unexpected pairing, convivial gathering

## KEY DIFFERENTIATORS

### 1. Exclusive "Dynamisation" Process
- Proprietary method that reveals complete botanical flavor
- Not fermentation, not added sugars, not synthetic aromas
- **Result:** Pure botanical expression

**Messaging:**
> "Notre procédé exclusif de 'dynamisation' révèle la pureté complète de chaque ingrédient botanique."

### 2. Certified Organic & Transparent
- Bio certifiée (FR-BIO-01, Agriculture UE)
- Zero added sugar, zero synthetic ingredients
- Minimal production footprint
- Ethical sourcing (e.g., Foster Farm partnership)

**Messaging:**
> "Bio certifiée. Sans gluten. Sans sulfites. Procédé naturel. Ingrédients vrais."

### 3. Premium Positioning
- Positioned as celebration beverage, not health drink
- €4.50-5.50 per 75cl bottle (premium price point)
- For moments that matter: apéritifs, dinners, events

**Messaging:**
> "Une boisson pour les moments de célébration authentique."

## DESIGN LAYOUT STANDARDS

### Whitespace & Spacing
- Margins: Generous (min. 2rem on desktop, 1.5rem mobile)
- Section padding: 3rem+ vertical spacing
- Element margins: 1rem+ between related items

### Typography Sizing (Responsive)

**Desktop:**
- H1 (Hero): 48-64px
- H2 (Section): 36-48px
- H3 (Subsection): 24-32px
- Body: 16-18px
- Small/captions: 14px

**Mobile:**
- H1: 32-40px
- H2: 24-32px
- H3: 18-24px
- Body: 16px
- Small: 13-14px

### Image & Media Standards

**Hero Image:**
- Full-width, high quality (2000px+ width)
- Bottle-focused or lifestyle apéritif scene
- Professional photography, warm tones

**Section Images:**
- Max width: 600px (in text flow)
- Aspect ratios: 16:9 for landscape, 1:1 for product
- High resolution (at least 1200px width)

**Icons/Decorations:**
- Minimal, subtle
- Botanical line drawings (optional, not required)
- Bubble motifs (understated, not prominent)

## WRITING GUIDELINES

### Tone Rules

✅ **DO:**
- Use poetic, sensory language (taste, aroma, texture, moment)
- Write from celebration/authenticity angle
- Use italic for descriptive copy
- Be specific about flavors and experiences
- Embrace the "zero compromis" philosophy

❌ **DON'T:**
- Use apologetic tone ("better than," "only if")
- Make health claims without context
- Describe as "alternative" or "substitute"
- Use corporate jargon or marketing clichés
- Over-explain the process in technical terms

### Copy Length Standards

- Hero tagline: 1-5 words
- Section headline: 3-8 words
- Subtitle/italic: 1-2 sentences (8-20 words)
- Body paragraph: 2-4 sentences
- CTA: 2-4 words

### Examples of Brand-Aligned Copy

**Strong (Poetic, Sensory):**
> "Quand l'herbacée franc rencontre l'épice subtile."
> "La pureté botanique révélée."
> "Pour les moments qui comptent."

**Weak (Apologetic, Generic):**
> "A great alternative to alcohol"
> "Healthier than soda"
> "Perfect if you're not drinking"

## BRAND GUARDRAILS

### What Stays Consistent
- Cream/beige background (all pages)
- Serif typography for headlines (all pages)
- Poetic, italic tone for descriptions (all copy)
- Premium product positioning (all messaging)
- "Zero compromis. 100% festif" philosophy (all campaigns)
- Celebration-focused moment framing (all CTAs)

### What Can Vary
- Layout/structure (landing page vs. product page vs. press kit)
- Photography style (lifestyle vs. product vs. process)
- Italic emphasis placement (flexible, contextual)
- Section order (as long as hierarchy is clear)

### Deviation Red Flags
- Using apologetic tone ("better than")
- Health/wellness framing (not a diet drink)
- "Alternative" or "substitute" messaging
- Corporate/marketing jargon
- Non-premium imagery or design
- Breaking from cream/beige/black color scheme
